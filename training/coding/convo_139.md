### Why Consider Schema Markup for Local SEO?
- **It's optional but beneficial**: It tells search engines exactly what your content means (e.g., "This is my business address and hours"). This can lead to enhanced search listings, like showing your phone number or reviews directly in results.
- **Low barrier to entry**: You don't need coding skills or fancy tools—just a free plugin or generator. It won't break your site if done wrong, and it's reversible.
